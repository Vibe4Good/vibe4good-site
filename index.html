Our responsible AI pledge

Build bold. Stay safe.

We open every session with these six habits.

  1. Use a sandbox account

    A separate Gmail for experiments while you learn. Real accounts only if you choose.

  2. Keep private stuff out

    No passwords, money, health details or other people’s info in AI chats.

  3. Read before you allow

    Know what a script wants to access, and why.

  4. Check your AI settings

    See whether your chats can be used to train models.

  5. You own the off switch

    Stop or delete anything you build, any time.

  6. AI can be wrong

    Check its work. You’re the human in charge.

Questions

Before you come

Do I need to know how to code?

No. Most people who come have never written code. The AI writes it; you describe what you want, and helpers walk the room if you get stuck.

Is it free?

Yes. Every session is free, and the tools we use have free versions.

What should I bring?

A charged laptop, a free sandbox Gmail made just for experimenting, and a free Claude or ChatGPT account. We’ll send setup steps before each session.

Who is it for?

Everyone: total beginners, seasoned developers, charity workers, students, the curious. Doors: everyone.
